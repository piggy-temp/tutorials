# 🍦 Our Modpack: Vanilla Perfected

For this server, we are using **Vanilla Perfected** by **demonjoeTV**. This pack is designed to enhance the game without making it feel like a completely different game (unlike those tech-heavy or magic-themed packs).

### ✨ What makes it "Perfected"?
The goal of this pack is to fix the "clunky" parts of Minecraft while keeping the core gameplay identical.
*   **Performance:** Drastic FPS boosts—it runs much smoother than vanilla.
*   **Atmosphere:** Subtle visual upgrades like splash effects, better leaves, and improved mob animations.
*   **Quality of Life:** Small tweaks like better inventory management, improved horse travel, and a modernized HUD.
*   **Vanilla Feel:** No "overpowered" machines or weird ores. The gameplay experience should be effectively the same as playing vanilla Minecraft, just faster/smoother.

## 📥 How to Download (1.21.11 Fabric)

> [!NOTE]
> Make sure you are searching for the **Modrinth** platform version on the **Fabric** mod loader for Minecraft version **1.21.11**.

### **Option 1: Prism Launcher**
1.  Open Prism and click **Add Instance** (top left).
2.  Click the **Modrinth** tab on the left sidebar.
3.  Search for `Vanilla Perfected`.
4.  Select the result by **demonjoeTV**.
5.  On the right side, click **Versions** and ensure you select the one labeled **1.21.11** (look for the release version if possible).
6.  Click **OK** to download.

### **Option 2: XMCL**
1.  Open XMCL and click the **Modpack Market** (store icon) on the left sidebar.
2.  Ensure the **SOURCE** at the bottom is set to **Modrinth** by clicking on FTB and CurseForge to cross them out.
3.  Search for `Vanilla Perfected` in the top bar.
4.  Click the result by **demonjoeTV**.
5.  Click the green **Install** button.
6.  In the version list that pops up, select the **Release** for **1.21.11**.
7.  Once finished, the pack will appear in your sidebar ready to launch.

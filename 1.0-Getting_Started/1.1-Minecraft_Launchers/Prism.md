# 💎 Setting Up Prism Launcher

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Make sure you have your Microsoft account details ready to log in once the app is installed.

1.  Go to the [Prism Download Page](https://prismlauncher.org/download/windows/).
2.  Click the **installer.exe** under the **x86-64** option.
3.  Run the `.exe` and follow the setup.

---

## 🔑 1. Log In
Before you can use Prism, you need to link your Microsoft account that owns the game:
1.  In the top right corner of the main window, click the **Profiles** button.
2.  Select **Manage Accounts**.
3.  Click **Add Microsoft** on the right side.
4.  A code will appear, and a browser window will open. Log in to your Microsoft account and enter the code if prompted.
5.  Once your account appears in the list, click **Close**.

---

## ⚡ 2. Global Performance Settings (RAM)
Instead of changing settings for every game you install, you can set "Global" defaults. This ensures every modpack you download has enough power to run smoothly.

1.  Click the **Settings** button in the top toolbar.
2.  Select **Java** from the left-hand sidebar.
3.  Look for the **Memory** section.
4.  Change the **Maximum Memory Usage** to **`6096 MiB`**.
    *   *Note: Our laptops have 16GB of RAM. Assigning 6GB (or 8GB if you're feeling "spicy") gives Minecraft plenty of room to breathe while leaving enough for the rest of your computer to run.*
5.  Click **Finish** or **OK**.

---

## 🎨 3. Customization & UI
As we discussed in the comparison, Prism is built for utility rather than beauty. It doesn't support full-blown themes or background images like XMCL, but you can still tidy it up:

*   **Icons:** You can change the icon of any instance by right-clicking it and selecting "Change Icon."
*   **Layout:** Under **Settings > UI**, you can change how your instances are displayed (Icons, List, or Compact) and toggle the dark mode theme.
*   **Simplicity:** Prism's strength is its "no-nonsense" design. Once you set your RAM and log in, you rarely ever have to look at these menus again.

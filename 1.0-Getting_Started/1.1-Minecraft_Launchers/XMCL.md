## 🔑 1. Log In
XMCL handles logins through your web browser for security, even though it looks like it's in-app.
1.  Click the **Login** button.
2.  It will appear as though you can log in within the app, but once you type in your username and click it, it will redirect you to your default browser.
3.  Sign in with your Microsoft account there, and the app will automatically sync.

---

## ⚡ 2. Set Global RAM Defaults
Instead of changing settings for every game you install, you can set a "Global" default so every new instance starts with enough power.

1.  Click the **Settings Cogwheel** on the far-left sidebar (at the very bottom).
2.  Look for the **Global Instance Settings** section (it looks like a box with a plus and two dots).
<img width="1020" height="760" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d0f24163-43ad-4af4-8d6a-ad620f214c46" />

3.  Scroll down to find **Java Memory**.
4.  Switch the toggle from "Unassigned" to **Manual** (the red button).
5.  Drag the red dotted bar until the maximum is between **6.0G** and **8.0G**. 
    *   *Note: Many laptops have 16GB of RAM. Assigning 6GB (or 8GB if you're feeling "spicy") gives Minecraft plenty of room to breathe while leaving enough for the rest of your computer to run.*
<img width="1020" height="760" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/500d64c0-f6dc-4ece-af6c-928fb9416a2b" />

## 🖼️ 3. Customize Your Global Default Theme
XMCL allows you to skin the launcher however you like. 

1.  Look for the **Appearance** section (it looks like a painter's palette).
2.  Scroll down to find **Theme Settings**.
4.  From here, you can:
    *   Upload a background image (like a Pank photo).
    *   Adjust the blur and saturation of the UI.
    *   Change the accent colors to match your aesthetic.
5.  When you actually download an instance you can toggle the **Instance Theme** switch to "On" to change it from the global default.

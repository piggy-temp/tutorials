# 📂 What are Instances?

In third-party launchers like XMCL and Prism, you don't just "play Minecraft." Instead, you create and play **Instances** of Minecraft.

Think of an **Instance** as a clone or a "mini-installation" of the game. Each instance has its own:
*   **Mods** (which won't interfere with other packs).
*   **Worlds** (your save files).
*   **Screenshots**.
*   **Settings** (keybinds, FOV, and video settings).

---

## ❓ Why use them?

### 1. No More "File Juggling"
In the default Minecraft launcher, every version of the game shares the same folder. If you try to switch from a Modded version to a Normal version, the files often clash, causing crashes or corrupted worlds. Instances keep everything **completely separate**.

### 2. Version Control
You can have one instance for Minecraft 1.21.1 (our modded server) and another for 1.8.9 (for mini-games) sitting side-by-side. You can switch between them with one click without ever having to move files around manually.

### 3. Total Organization
Because each instance has its own folder, your screenshots and worlds won't get mixed up with your creative mode testing or other modpacks. It’s cleaner, safer, and much harder to accidentally break your game.

# 🛠️ XMCL vs. Prism: Choosing Your Launcher

The default Minecraft launcher kinda sucks for anything that isn't just default Minecraft and even then it's not particularly stand out... If you want things like mods or shaders and other things of the like it requires a ton of behind-the-scenes file managing and it's a major headache. Realistically, you want a **third-party launcher**.

There's pretty much two best options right now: **XMCL** and **Prism**. Both are _open-source_, _lightweight_, and _trustworthy_.

---

## 📊 Comparison at a Glance

| Feature | **XMCL** | **Prism** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vibe** | Modern, flashy, and feature-rich. | Minimalist, "Old Reliable." |
| **Ease of Use** | Slightly higher learning curve (5-10 clicks). | Extremely simple (3-5 clicks). |
| **Customization** | Full control over themes (images/UI). | Very little; it’s built for utility. |
| **Performance** | Very lightweight/efficient. | Solid and consistent. |

---

## ❓ Which one should you pick?

### **Pick XMCL if...**
You plan on playing Minecraft more than a few times a month. Some people claim it’s a bit "overtuned" with features because it allows for total customization, but they do a lot of secret backend coding magic that kinda proves they really care about performance too in ways that others don't seem to be thinking of. If you want your launcher to look pretty (like my custom Pank theme) and enjoy having more control over your setup, this is the one.

### **Pick Prism if...**
You just want to play once or twice and don't want to think about it. It’s widely considered the "gold standard" for stability. It might not be the prettiest, but it’s fast, simple, and gets the job done without any fluff. If you just straight Google "best minecraft launcher" this is the one 99% of replies are picking.

# ⚙️ Recommended Game Settings

Modpacks can be heavy. To ensure the game looks good but runs smoothly on a laptop, I recommend these specific settings.

### 👁️ General

* **FOV:** `90` (The sweet spot between "tunnel vision" and "fish-eye").

### 📺 Video Settings

* **GUI Scale:** `Auto` (Usually the first tick).
* **Fullscreen:** `Yes`.
* **Max Framerate:**
* `60 FPS` if you are lagging or your fans are screaming.
* `90 - 120 FPS` for smoother performance if your laptop can handle it.



### ✨ Graphics & Quality

* **Graphics:** `Fast`.
* **Clouds/Weather/Leaves:** `Fast` or `Off`.
* **Particles:** `Decreased`. (Some mods need particles to show you what's happening, so don't turn them completely off!)

### ⌨️ Controls & Keybinds

* **Sneak:** `Left Control`.
* **Sprint:** `Left Shift`.
* **Auto-Jump:** `OFF`. (This is mandatory—auto-jump is the enemy).
* **Sprint/Crouch:** Set to `Hold` (unless you prefer `Toggle` for casual play).
* **Inventory Sorting:** Search for "sort" in the keybinds and set **Sort Inventory** to `Middle Click` (Mouse Wheel).

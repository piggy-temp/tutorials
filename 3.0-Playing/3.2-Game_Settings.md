# ⚙️ Recommended Game Settings

Modpacks can be heavy. To ensure the game looks good but runs smoothly on a laptop, I recommend these specific settings. Also thrown in are just some random settings I like to use. These settings are all the default settings I set every single time I open a new Minecraft instance pretty much no matter what even with my powerful desktop PC. 

### 👁️ General

* **FOV:** `90` (FOV is your Field of Vision. 90 is the most common other than default and is considered the sweet spot between "tunnel vision" and "fish-eye").

### 📺 Video Settings

* **GUI Scale:** `Auto` (Usually the first or last tick/option).
* **Fullscreen:** `Yes`(I'd use borderless if it's available but that's not common).
* **Max Framerate:**
    * `60 FPS` (if you are lagging or your fans are screaming).
    * `90 - 120 FPS` (for smoother performance if your laptop can handle it).



### ✨ Graphics & Quality

* **Graphics:** `Fast`.
* **Clouds/Weather/Leaves:** `Fast` or `Off` (If you're going to be climbing mountains or building towers it may be nice to see the clouds pass by or if your shaders are nice it may be nice to look up into the sky at them). 
* **Particles:** `Decreased`. (Some mods need particles to show you what's happening, so don't turn them completely off unless you're sure none of these mods are installed or you just care more about performance than playability which is also valid for lower end computers).

### ⌨️ Controls & Keybinds

* **Sneak:** `Left Control` (This is just for comfort since other games are like this).
* **Sprint:** `Left Shift` (This is just for comfort since other games are like this).
* **Auto-Jump:** `OFF`. (This is mandatory. Auto-jump is the enemy).
* **Sprint/Crouch:** Set to `Hold` (unless you prefer `Toggle` for casual/lazy play).
* **Inventory Sorting:** Search for "sort" in the keybinds and set **Sort Inventory** to `Middle Click` (Mouse Wheel Button).
